# Spec: the post feed (MY-261)

Branch `my-261-post-feed`. What a visitor sees and does; the build is in `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-10-28-post-feed.md`.
The feed is built as a component and shown on `/ui` (a blog feed and a news feed); the Home and News sections that
use it arrive with their own tasks.

## The row

- A post row has three parts: the author (a round avatar, the name, the post's category), the text (the date, the
  title, the excerpt, then the likes, comments and shares counters) and a "View Blog" button that goes to the
  article. The title is a heading; the button's name for assistive technology is "View Blog about <title>".
- From 768 px up the parts sit on one line (author column fixed, text taking the rest, button at the right).
  Below 768 px the author and the button share the first line and the text follows under them. Rows are separated
  by a 1px line; the sizes at 1920, 1440 and 390 are the design's.

## The tabs

- Above the list a row of tabs: "All" and one per category. "All" is selected first and the list shows every post.
- A click, or the arrow keys, `Home` and `End` on the focused tab, select a tab: the list then shows only the posts
  of that category, at once and without a reload. `Tab` moves into the tab bar on the selected tab only and out of
  it to the list's first link; the arrows wrap around.
- The selected tab is filled and white with a heavier label. At 1440 and wider the tabs are equal and fill the
  width; below that the bar scrolls sideways and a selected tab scrolls into view.
- A category with no post shows "No posts yet" in place of the list; the tab stays selectable.
- How many rows the feed holds is a prop: only that many posts are rendered, tabs filter within them.

## What a screen reader hears

- The bar is a tab list named "Post categories"; every tab controls the one list panel, and the panel is named by
  the selected tab.
- Each selection is announced politely with the category and the count: "AI Ethics: 1 post", "All: 3 posts",
  "Biotechnology: no posts yet". Nothing is announced when the page loads.

## Without JavaScript

- Every post shows in the order of the data, and the tab bar is not shown (it cannot work), as the play buttons
  and the header menu already behave without scripting.
