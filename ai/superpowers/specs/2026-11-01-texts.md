# Spec: texts and UX labels (MY-325)

Branch `my-325-texts`. The table of every string is in `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-texts.md`.

## Behaviour

- No button promises a file. Where the site once said "Download PDF Now", it says "Request the PDF" and still opens the
  contact form; the Home groups say "Browse Ebooks" / "Browse Whitepapers" and "View Details", and open that tab of
  Resources.
- A "View All" says what it opens: "View All News" opens News, "View All Podcasts" opens Podcasts, "View Blogs" opens the
  blog feed on Home.
- Opening a post is "Read More" on every card (News, Featured, the Home and News feeds, Similar News). Playing a show or
  an episode is "Listen Now".
- The contact form opens with "United States (+1)" selected and its flag shown; a number needs 10 digits, and the error
  says "Enter 10 digits for United States." The country list keeps working: another country changes the flag, the pattern
  and the error as before.
- The form's placeholders read "Enter your …" for every field.
- The CTA says "FutureTech"; the 404 tab title is "Page Not Found".

## Layout

- No button label wraps and nothing scrolls sideways at 390, 1440 and 1920; the buttons keep their size, apart from the
  width their new text needs.
