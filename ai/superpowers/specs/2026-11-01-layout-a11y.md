# MY-313: layout and accessibility behavior

Plan: `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-layout-a11y.md`.

## What a user sees

- FAQ questions, the consent text and the required mark have no letter spacing, as in the design (`ls 0` rows of
  `ai/design-system.md`); headings and body text keep -3% of their own size. The country select matches the other fields.
- Contact form at 320px: the fields fit the card, no part leaves it, the page has no horizontal scroll at 320, 390, 768,
  1024, 1440 and 1920px.
- Stat numbers keep their width while counting; the "+" does not move.
- Pressing a button, a tab or the like toggle shows a short darker step (`:active`), on a touch screen as well as with
  a mouse. A disabled like (no script) has the default cursor and no press look.
- No visual change in any other place: drawn sizes and paddings are the design's.

## Touch and pointer

- Every control in the list has a hit area of at least 44px in each direction on its own: the FAQ question, small
  buttons and footer pills, header links, the logo, footer social and source icons, footer links.
- Where controls sit closer than 44px (stacked footer links, the footer social row) the area of each ends where the next
  one's begins, so a tap on the gap picks the later one; the layout does not change to make room.

## What a screen reader hears

- An article has the landmarks `banner`, `navigation` (x2), `main`, `contentinfo` and nothing else: its sections are
  no longer nine unnamed regions; their `h2` headings stay.
- Headings are the page's sections: the hero promo cards and the footer columns are no longer `h2`; the podcast tagline
  is no longer `h4`.
- A resource row's title is read once (its `h3`), not again in the panel beside the photo.
