# Spec: the header (MY-257)

Branch `my-257-header`. What a visitor sees and does; the build is in `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-10-28-header.md`.

## Parts, top to bottom

1. **Skip link** "Skip to content": the first focusable thing on every page, hidden until it takes focus, then a
   primary button in the top-left corner. `Enter` moves focus to `main#main`.
2. **Newsletter strip**: "Subscribe to our Newsletter For New & latest Blogs and Resources" and the yellow
   arrow, centred, one link to `/contact`. Below 768 px the words "New & latest" are left out, as the Mobile
   design does.
3. **Navbar**: the logo (a link to `/`, named "FutureTech") on the left; Home, News, Podcasts, Resources
   centred on the page; Contact Us (primary button) on the right. Both bars end in a 1px line.

## Active page

- The item whose address is the current page has `aria-current="page"` and is drawn as a plate: `Dark/08`
  fill, 1px `Dark/20` border, white medium label. The other links are bare grey text.
- Contact Us on `/contact` also carries `aria-current="page"` and keeps the button look. Pages that are in no
  menu item (404, `/ui`, later articles) highlight nothing.

## Widths

- From 768 px up the full navbar shows; the sizes follow the design at 1920 and 1440 and move between them.
- Below 768 px the navbar is the logo and a menu button (the 34 px glyph, white). The links and Contact Us are
  not in the bar; they live in the menu.

## Mobile menu

- The button has the name "Open menu", `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Activating it opens a drawer that
  slides in from the right over the page, which is dimmed behind it. The drawer holds a "Close menu" button
  (the close glyph, at the position of the menu button), the links (the active one as a plate, like on desktop)
  and Contact Us.
- While it is open the page behind does not scroll and cannot be reached by keyboard or by assistive
  technology; focus starts on "Close menu" and `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` reach the drawer's controls only.
- It closes on `Escape`, on a click on the dimmed area, on "Close menu" and on a click on a link. Focus returns
  to the menu button, except after a link that leaves the page.
- Widening the window to 768 px or more closes it and shows the full navbar.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it opens and closes without movement.

## Without JavaScript

The menu button, the dimmed area and "Close menu" are not shown; below 768 px the bar shows the logo, and the
links and Contact Us below it as a plain list.
