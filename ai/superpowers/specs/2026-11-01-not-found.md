# Spec: the 404 page (MY-310)

Branch `my-310-not-found`. What a visitor sees and does; the build is in
`ai/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-not-found.md`.

## Page

- Any address the site does not have, and `/404`, shows the title "Error 404: Page Not Found" as the page's one `h1`, set
  like the titles of News, Podcasts and Resources: two lines, the second beside a short text, stacked below 768px. The
  gaps and the side padding are the same as on those pages at every width.
- Under the text, aligned with it, is the primary button "Back to Home" with the arrow icon; it is a link to `/`.
- The header, the CTA and the footer stay as on every page.

## Keyboard and head

- Tab reaches the button after the header's controls, the focus ring is the site's; Enter opens the Home page.
- The page stays out of search: `noindex`, and it is not in the sitemap. Its title and description do not change.

## Layout

- No horizontal scroll from 390px up.
