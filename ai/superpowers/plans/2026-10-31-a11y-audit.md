## Audit

- axe-core (the version `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` already installs, run from `node_modules`, nothing added to `package.json`)
  in headless Chrome over CDP against `npm run preview`: every page and every article at 1440 and 390, tags WCAG 2.0, 2.1
  and 2.2 A/AA plus best practice, after scrolling the page so reveal has run. Target: 0 violations.
- Keyboard: Tab through every page at both widths (order against the layout, visible ring, ring not clipped by an
  `overflow` ancestor, every control reached), the drawer and the video modal (focus in, Tab, `Escape`, focus back).
- Headings and landmarks: one `h1`, no skipped level, `header` / `nav` / `main` / `footer`, names on repeated landmarks.
- Accessibility tree (`Accessibility.getFullAXTree`): tabs, accordion, the form with errors, the video modal, the table of
  contents. The modal is opened only with the iframe `src` forced to `about:blank` and the video hosts blocked.
- Text over photos is measured on pixels (text hidden, screenshot, worst contrast of the pixels under the text).

## Contrast (the author's decision)

- `--grey-50` is text only: raised to the least value that reaches 4.5:1 on `Dark/10`, the darkest-contrast background it
  sits on. Text that used `--dark-40` (footer, eyebrow, places, placeholders) takes it; `--dark-40` stays for the
  hover borders, the avatar ring and the counter icon (3:1).
- `--border-control` (a 1px `--dark-40` line, 3:1 on `Dark/10`) replaces `--border` on the form fields, the phone country box
  and the checkbox. Every other border keeps `--border`.
- The video duration over a photo becomes white: no grey passes on every photo.
- A new dated record with the before and after ratios; the Contrast table of `ai/design-system.md` follows.
