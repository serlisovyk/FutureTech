Branch `my-282-seo`, after MY-282. No spec: no page looks or behaves differently. Records cited: `shared-page-head`,

## robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt

- One plugin, `vite/plugins/site-files.js`, registered in `vite/parts/plugins.js`. In `generateBundle` it reads the pages
  minista has built (`fileName` ends in `.html`) and emits three assets: `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`.
  It does nothing in a bundle without pages.
- The pages are the source: a page's URL comes from its file (`index.html` is `/`, `blog/<slug>.html` is `/blog/<slug>`:
  the clean URLs `vercel.json` serves), and its title, description, `robots` and `article:published_time` are read back
  from the head the layout printed. So a page or a post added later is in the three files without touching them, and a
  page's text in `llms.txt` is the text the page carries. The origin is `site.url`.
- A page is left out of the sitemap and `llms.txt` and disallowed in `robots.txt` when it is `404.html` or has
  `noindex` (`/ui`). `/ui` keeps its `noindex` meta: the two are belt and braces.
- `sitemap.xml`: `<loc>` per page, `<lastmod>` from `article:published_time` for an article, none for the others (no
  honest date). `llms.txt` (llmstxt.org shape): the site name, the Home description as the summary, a Pages list and an
  Articles list, each a link with the page's description. Generated, because it lists the articles.
- Helpers live in `vite/site-files/`, one function per file: `read-pages`, `build-robots`, `build-sitemap`, `build-llms`.

## JSON-LD

- `Organization` on every page (`name`, `url`, `logo` = the apple-touch icon), `BlogPosting` on an article (`headline`,
  `description`, `datePublished`, `author` as a `Person`, `image`, `publisher` = the same organization,
  `mainEntityOfPage`), built in `utils/`, serialized with `<` escaped, printed by the layout in `<script type="application/ld+json">`.
- No `sameAs`: the footer's social links are the networks' home pages, not the site's profiles. No `BreadcrumbList`: the
  site shows no breadcrumbs, and markup must match what the page shows.
