# Plan: the post feed (MY-261)

Branch `my-261-post-feed`. Spec: `ai/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-post-feed.md`. Records cited: `folders-and-barrels`
(where components live), `borders-inside` (the row, tab and bar borders), `fluid-ramps` (every size), `oop-javascript`
and `mobile-menu-drawer` (the `scripting: none` fallback), `cards-and-modal-size-limits` (the budget). minista API:
none new; the feed is plain JSX that sections render, `Photo` and `Icon` are the existing wrappers.

## Where things live (decided here)

- `PostRow` is `components/cards/PostRow/`: a repeated element like the cards, one `<article>`, no knowledge of
  the feed. `PostFeed` is `components/feeds/PostFeed/`, a new group by role: Home and News render the same
  construction with other categories and data, so it is a component the two sections compose, not a section.
  A section keeps its own heading band and passes the feed its props.
- `Tabs` is extended by one hook and the feed rides on it. `PostFeedController` (`scripts/controllers/`) is the
  section-level class: it owns a `Tabs` for the feed root, filters the rows and writes the status, so the tab logic
  (selection, roving `tabindex`, arrows, `Home`, `End`) is not repeated.

## Markup contract

- Root `data-post-feed`, no `data-tabs` (`main.js` would create a second `Tabs`). Inside: the tab list
  `role="tablist" data-tab-list` with `aria-label` from data; each tab `<button role="tab" data-tab="<slug>"
data-status="<announcement>">` with its id, `aria-controls` the one panel, `aria-selected` (true on "All") and
  `tabindex` (0 on "All", -1 on the rest).
- One panel `role="tabpanel"` with an id and `aria-labelledby` the initial tab; it has no `data-tab-panel` (that
  hook is for one panel per tab and `Tabs` would hide it). It holds the `<ul>` of rows, each
  `<li data-category="<slug>">` with a `PostRow`, and the empty text `<p data-post-feed-empty hidden>`.
- A visually hidden `<p role="status" data-post-feed-status>` after the bar: the polite live region.
- The announcement of each tab is computed at build time (`getPostFeedStatus(count, categoryName)`), so the script
  holds no plural logic and no text: it copies `data-status` of the selected tab into the live region.
- Without JavaScript nothing is hidden by markup except the empty text (shown by the build only when no post is
  rendered); `@media (scripting: none)` hides the tab bar.

## Build

- `Tabs`: `onSelect(handler)` like `Toggle.onToggle`: the handler gets the selected tab after every selection,
  including the first one in `start()`. Nothing else changes.
- `PostFeedController`: handler registered before `tabs.start()`; on a selection it shows the rows whose
  `data-category` equals the key (all rows for the "All" key), shows the empty text when no row is visible,
  points the panel's `aria-labelledby` at the selected tab, and writes the status, except for the selection `start()`
  makes (a flag set once started) so the load announces nothing. `main.js`: one row `[data-post-feed]`.
- `scripts/constants.js`: the "All" key and the hooks the controller reads.
- `PostFeed` props: `id` (makes the tab and panel ids unique per feed), `categories`, `posts`, `limit` (default: all
  posts). It renders the first `limit` posts; the status counts are of those. `PostRow` props: `slug`, `title`,
  `excerpt`, `date`, `category`, `authorName`, `authorAvatar`, `likes`, `comments`, `shares`.
- `utils/` (one function per file): `formatDate` (the long US date, UTC), `getPostsInCategory(posts, slug)`,
  `getPostFeedStatus(count, categoryName)`, `getPostFeedTabId(feedId, slug)`; their numbers and formats in
  `utils/constants.js`. `data/`: `post-feed.js` (the tab list label, the empty text, the announcement
  templates), `categories.js` exports the `All` entry once, `card-photos.js` gets the avatar `sizes` and widths,
  `ui-kit.js` the two demos (feed id, categories, placement, limit).
- Layout: the row is a grid, author | text | button, gap 50 / 40; below 1024 px (a side-by-side composition) two rows (author and button, then
  text), gap 30. Block padding is `--space-section` (80 / 60 / 40) minus the border; the bar scrolls sideways
  and its tabs are equal grid columns of at least 152 px, their label centred and allowed to overflow the padding
  as the design draws it. The shared sizes come from existing tokens; the avatar, the row title, the date and the
  tab sizes are written in the block. `CounterPill` carries the counters (heart, comment, send), `Button` the link.
- `/ui`: two groups, "PostRow and PostFeed": the blog categories with the `blogs` posts (three rows) and the news
  categories with the `headlines` posts, limited to two rows. Both show an empty category.

## Deviations from the design (reported)

- The first row's title on Home Desktop is grey where every other row's is white: white everywhere.
- The first pill of the first Home and News rows is drawn liked: not built; a like is the visitor's own state and the
  first row changes with the filter.
- "No posts yet" and the status line are not in the design.
- Two posts rendered here (the `blogs` and `headlines` ones with the third author) pointed at portraits that are not
  in `assets/images/portraits/`; they now use two of the four that exist. The unrendered posts keep their paths.
