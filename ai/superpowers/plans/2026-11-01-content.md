## Decisions taken in the plan

- **News hub feeds all News posts** (placements `featured`, `trending`, `headlines`), not only the three `headlines`: four
  tabs would need four categories, and the three headline posts are all Technology. Adding posts needs new photos, which the
  images budget does not allow. The hub becomes the archive of the page; the Sports tab goes, no post is about sports.
- **The flagship article goes to the Home blog feed**, as a `biotechnology` blog (its sections are diagnostics, drug discovery,
  treatment). Home already says "In-Depth Blog Posts" over three-section posts; this is the one long one. The Home feed shows
  four posts instead of three, so each of its tabs has a post; the Renewable Energy tab goes, no blog post is about it. The
  `article` placement value is removed.
- **Footer category links select the tab.** The feeds do not read a tab from the hash today (`Tabs` matches the panel id, and the
  feed has one panel). One line in `Tabs` also matches a tab's own `id`, so `/news#news-hub-feed-tab-health` selects Health on
  load and on `hashchange`; the browser's own jump scrolls to the tab. Without the script the link still lands on the tab
  list. This is the only script change; it is its own commit and can be dropped, then the footer category links fall back to
  the section ids.
- **Authors become a registry** `data/authors.js`: an object of `{ name, avatar }`, one entry per person; a post names its author
  with `author: authors.<key>`, so the shape the components read does not change.
- **Numbers**: one picture. 150+ resources = Over 100 ebooks + Over 50 whitepapers (the Reports tab is a short list of featured
  reports and is not counted); 1,000+ articles is the archive; the 6 entries of the library are its highlights.
- **Socials**: only the author's GitHub profile is real, so the three icons become one. The brand roots (`twitter.com/`,
  `medium.com/`, `linkedin.com/`) go with their unused icons.
- **Contact data** use the reserved domain `futuretech.example` and the NANP fiction range `555-0100` to `555-0199`.
