# Plan: the News hero and the featured story (MY-268)

Branch `my-268-news-hero`. No spec: both blocks have no behavior, they are markup, styles and one link. Records cited:
`folders-and-barrels` (where the blocks live), `borders-inside` (the line above the story), `fluid-ramps` (every size),
`image-formats` (the photo goes through `Photo`). minista API: none new; `Photo` already passes `loading` and
`fetchPriority` to the `<img>`.

## Where things live (decided here)

- `components/page/PageIntro/` (`PageIntro.jsx`, `PageIntro.css`, `index.jsx`) in a new group `components/page/` (page-level
  blocks; `components/index.jsx` re-exports it). It is not in `ui/`: it holds the page's `h1`, so it cannot be shown on `/ui`,
  which has its own. A page renders it directly with its data, like a section; Podcasts and Resources reuse it.
- `sections/news/FeaturedNews/` (`FeaturedNews.jsx`, `FeaturedNews.css`, `index.jsx`); a new group barrel
  `sections/news/index.jsx`, re-exported by `sections/index.jsx`.
- `data/news.js`: `newsIntro` (title id, the two title lines, the paragraph) and `featuredNews` (title id, placement
  `featured`, the labels of the three meta pairs). The story itself is the post with `placement: 'featured'`
  (`getPostsByPlacement`), so its text, date, author and counters have one source. `data/card-photos.js` gets the
  `sizes` and the widths of the story photo.
- `pages/news.jsx` keeps `metadata`, drops the placeholder and renders `<PageIntro … />` and `<FeaturedNews />`.
- The photo is `assets/images/photos/wind-turbines-sunset.jpg`, the name `data/posts.js` already uses (JPEG q92 4:4:4,
  source 626x417: the design's own file, never upscaled).

## Layout

- `PageIntro`: a `section` labelled by its `h1` (`Heading`, `size="display"`). The design breaks the title into two lines
  and puts the paragraph to the right of the second one, centred on it (gap 80 / 60). One `h1` in the DOM with two line
  spans: the section is a two-column grid, the `h1` a subgrid across both columns and both rows, the first line spans
  both columns, the second line and the paragraph share the second row. Under 768 px it is a plain block: the title
  wraps by itself, the paragraph under it (gap 14). Block padding 130 / 80 / 40 above and 150 / 100 / 40 below.
- `FeaturedNews`: a `section` with a line above it, block padding `--space-section`, a grid `[photo | body]` (photo
  `min(515px, 40%)`, gap 80 / 40 / 30), stacked under 1024 px with the photo above. Photo height 427 / 325 / 213, radius
  `--radius-card`, `object-fit: cover`. The body is a column: title (lead title 32 / 24 / 20, weight 600) and paragraph
  (`--font-size-subheading`), then `MetaGrid` (Category, Publication Date, Author; gap 50 / 30 / 20), then the counters
  (`CounterPill`: likes, comments) at the start and the outline `Button` "Read More" to the article at the end.
- Heading order: `h1`, then the story's `h2`.
- `MetaGrid` gets a `className` (the section sets the gap and the text gap of its pairs the design draws here).

## Deviations from the design (reported)

- The design's meta values are Regular at 1920 and White at the other widths; the kit's `MetaItem` value is Medium White:
  kept. The design's pairs sit 2 px apart at 1440 and 390 (4 at 1920); the kit's gap is the one of the Blog meta cells,
  so the section overrides it to the design's.
