## Tokens

Names are lowercase kebab-case; a colour is named like the design's style, a size by its role.

- **Colours (11 styles the pages use + 1):** `--white`, `--dark-08`, `--dark-10`, `--dark-15`, `--dark-20`,
  `--dark-25`, `--dark-40`, `--grey-50`, `--grey-60`, `--grey-80`, `--yellow-55`, and `--color-like`
  (`#FF5500`, the filled heart). Stars are `--yellow-55` (not `#FFCE22`); the `#E5EFFF` separators are
  `--dark-15`. No semantic aliases: one value, one name; the role of each colour is the table in
  `ai/design-system.md`. `#D9D9D9` (flag), `#9C9C9C` (hero ray) and `#FFD119` (end of the "New" border)
  are drawn inside a single block each: the block that needs one adds it to the colour list.
- **Ramps:** `--ramp-390-1440`, `--ramp-1440-1920` exactly as in the record.
- **Fonts:** `--font-kumbh`, `--font-inter`, each with `system-ui, sans-serif` behind it. Named by face, not
  by role: Inter also sets titles (card, row, article) and Kumbh Sans also sets numbers.
- **Motion:** `--transition-duration` (0.2s). The design draws no hover or focus state, so this is the one
  value not taken from it; reduced motion sets it to `0s` (below).
- **Type steps, `--font-size-*`**, fluid, written from the design numbers (1920 / 1440 / 390), taken where
  the design-system table gives the same step to two or more blocks: `display` 80 / 55 / 28 (page titles),
  `h2` 58 / 44 / 28, `h3` 40 / 30 / 24 (feature, Ebooks, Whitepapers), `body` 18 / 16 / 14 (paragraph, value,
  quote, FAQ answer), `lead` 20 / 16 / 14 (lead, tag, meta value), `card-title` 22 / 18 / 16 (CTA, episode,
  resource cards), `button` 18 / 14 / 14 (buttons, tabs, footer link, field text). Line heights
  `--line-height-body` 1.5 and `--line-height-heading` 1.3; letter spacing `--letter-spacing` -0.03em.
- **Spacing, `--space-*`:** `container` 162 / 80 / 16 (page side padding, also list row, tab bar, navbar);
  `section` 80 / 60 / 40 (list row, split-row panel, footer, form block padding); `band` 120 / 80 / 40
  (heading band block padding); `card` 40 / 30 / 24 (feature and CTA card padding).
- **Radii, `--radius-*`:** `card` 12 / 10 / 10 (cards, trays, images, quote card), `control` 8 / 6 / 6
  (ghost and download buttons, field, footer link button), `pill` 100px.
- **Border:** `--border: 1px solid var(--dark-15)`, the one border of the design.

Anything else the design gives a single block is written in that block's stylesheet (`ai/rules/css.md`).

## Decided here

- **Reduced motion without `!important`:** `css.md` forbids it and a universal `transition: none` would lose
  to any component rule. A duration always comes from `--transition-duration`, so under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` `reset.css` sets that token to `0s` on `:root`: every transition and
  animation that follows the rule becomes instant, and no component writes its own query. Smooth scroll is
  never turned on, so there is nothing to turn off.
- **Letter spacing:** `em` is resolved where the property is declared, so `letter-spacing: -0.03em` on
  `body` would pass the body's -0.54px down to an 80px title. The rule is
  `*, ::before, ::after { letter-spacing: var(--letter-spacing) }` (zero specificity), so each element
  resolves it at its own size; "ls 0" rows set `letter-spacing: 0` in their block.
- **Base headings:** `h1` is the `display` step (page titles on three pages; the Home hero title 70 / 55 / 30
  overrides it in its block), `h2` the `h2` step, `h3` the `h3` step (K600, line height 1.5); h1 and h2 use
  Kumbh Sans 500 at line height 1.3. Headings are White, body text is Grey/60 on Dark/08.
- **Focus ring:** `outline: 2px solid var(--yellow-55)` with a 2px offset. Focus is not drawn in the design
  (`ai/design-system.md`); the yellow is the issue's, the 2px / 2px is the choice made here.
- **Dividers:** no utility. A divider is `--border` on the full-width section element and the content sits in
  a `.container` inside it, so the line ignores the inset by construction; one line in `css.md`.
- **`.container`:** `width: 100%` and `padding-inline: var(--space-container)`; no maximum width and no
  centring, as the design draws none (`ai/design-system.md`, section 1).
- **Reset content:** `box-sizing`, zero margins, lists without markers, media as blocks with `max-width`,
  form controls inheriting the font, links inheriting colour without underline, buttons without chrome.
