# Plan: the data in `src/data/`

Issue MY-251, branch `my-251-data`. No observable behavior changes (nothing imports the data yet), so no spec.

## Scope

- `src/data/` only: plain `.js` modules, one file per entity, named exports of plain values, no comments, no
  functions (a slug or URL helper belongs to the article-route task), kebab-case file names.
- Docs: `ai/map.md` (`data/`), a decision record for the article format and its index line.

Not in scope: any section, component or page that reads the data; copying the photo files (each section task
copies the files it needs); formatting helpers (a counter `24500` → "24.5k", an ISO date → "October 15, 2023").

## Decided

- **Article bodies are JS sections, no MDX** — the author's decision in the issue; record
  `2026-10-28-article-data`. A post has `body: [{ id, title, paragraphs }]`; the table of contents and the
  anchors come from `id` and `title`.
- **One export per file named like the file**, except where several sections read different shapes of one
  entity: `categories.js` (`blogCategories`, `newsCategories`: two tab bars with different sets, each with its own
  `All`; one list would need a filter in both sections and a double `All`), `features.js` (`heroFeatures`,
  `featureGroups`), `resources.js` (`resourceShowcases`, `resourceTabs`, `resourceCards`).
- **Which post goes where** is a plain field `placement`: `blogs` (the Home rows), `featured` (the News lead
  story), `trending` (the News cards, also the article's Similar News), `headlines` (the News hub rows), `article`
  (the design's open article, listed nowhere). The order in `posts` is the order of the design within a placement.
  A flag plus slices of one array, or separate slug lists, were weighed and rejected: the cards and the hub rows are
  both `news` and neither is the latest by date.
- **Counters** are numbers: `{ likes, comments, shares, views }`. The design draws a speech bubble as the second pill
  of a card and an eye on the article page, so the issue's "views" and the card's comments are both kept. A figure
  the design does not show is invented.
- **Category** of a post is a `slug` from the two lists. The design's article says "Healthcare", which is in no tab
  bar; it gets `health` ("Health", the news tab) so every category a post uses exists in a list.
- **Author** is nested in the post: `{ name, avatar }`. The line under the name in a row is the post's category, so
  there is no separate role. Avatars are the catalogue's portraits, reused (named by look, not by person).
- **Images** are root paths `/src/assets/images/<name>.<ext>` by the catalogue's names; `.png` for the portraits
  drawn with alpha, `.jpg` for the rest (as `healthcare-lab-hero.jpg`). A post has `imageAlt`; the other photos are
  decorative next to their title and carry no alt in data. A post with no photo in the design takes one from the
  catalogue.
- **Links**: root-relative clean URLs; a footer link into a page section is `/<page>#<id>`, and the section task gives
  the section that `id`: Home `features`, `blogs`, `resources`, `testimonials`; News `trending-stories`, `news-hub`,
  `featured-videos`; Podcasts `top-podcasts`, `latest-podcasts`; the Resources tab panels `whitepapers`, `ebooks`,
  `reports`. Terms and Privacy have no pages (`ai/rules/scope.md`), so those two links carry a label and no `href`.
  The design draws no target for the strip arrows of the Home hero (`heroFeatures`: `/news`, `/podcasts`,
  `/resources`) and none for "Newsletter" (`/contact`); the social links point at the networks' home pages, "Get
  Directions" at a map search for the address: all invented, for the author to confirm.
- **Icons** are the file names in `assets/icons/` (`feature-news`, …), read by `<Icon name>`.
- **`socials.js`** is a module of its own: the three icons repeat in the footer and in Contact.
- **`stats.js`** is one list of four counters: Home shows the first three, Resources all four.
- **Video id** is the string `placeholder-video-id`, filled by the video-modal task.
- **Placeholders** the design repeats (equal durations, equal card texts, the second "AI Revolution" in the footer,
  one email and phone for two contact blocks, the FAQ answers but one) are replaced by distinct plausible texts;
  a text the design cuts off is finished; "Ask Qustion" style typos are not copied.
