## Letter spacing

- Cause: `-0.03em` sits on `*, ::before, ::after`, so each element gets it itself and a parent's `letter-spacing: 0`
  never reaches its children (FAQ question, checkbox text, required mark).
- The issue suggests moving it to `body` and inheriting. That fails: an `em` letter-spacing is inherited as the
  computed length, so every heading would take the body's -0.48px instead of its own 3%.
- Instead the value is a custom property, which inherits as text and resolves against each element's own font size:
  `--letter-spacing: -0.03em` in the variables stylesheet, `letter-spacing: var(--letter-spacing)` on the base
  `*, ::before, ::after` rule, and a block that is `ls 0` in `ai/design-system.md` sets `--letter-spacing: 0` in place
  of `letter-spacing: 0` (Button primary, ConsentField label, status line, FormError, FormField label and control,
  FaqItem button and text, TestimonialCard name, place and text, Header link). The `::after` of `.header__link` loses its
  `letter-spacing: inherit`.
- The phone `select` gets the field's `ls 0` too (design: input text). Checked against the `ls 0` rows of the design
  system by computed style on `/contact` and `/`.
- One record, `letter-spacing-token`, and one line in `css.md`.

## Hit areas (44px)

An invisible `::before` / `::after` on the control, the drawn size unchanged (`css.md`). Measured with
`elementFromPoint` from the centre outwards; a pseudo-element overlapping a neighbour's loses to the later one in DOM
order, so two controls closer than 44px share the gap.

- FAQ question button: `::before`, `inset-block: -13px` (20px to 46px).
- `.button--small`: `::before`, `inset-block: -4px` (37px to 45px; `::after` of the primary is taken).
- `.header__link`: `::before` (`::after` holds the bold width reservation), `inset-block: -2px` (41px to 45px).
- `.header__logo`: `::after`, `inset-block: -5px` (35 to 45 at 390).
- `.footer__social`, `.footer__source`: `inset: -12px` (20px to 44px).
- `.footer__link`: `inset-block: -13px`, `inset-inline: -4px` (width of the shortest word, 36px, to 44px).
- Not reachable: the rows of footer links (pitch 26 to 36px) and the footer social icons (pitch 34 to 40px) are closer
  than 44px, so each area is capped by the pitch; the layout of the design stays. Reported, not hidden.
