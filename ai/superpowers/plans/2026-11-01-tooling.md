## 5. PostCSS plugins (`vite/postcss/`; `fluid.js` is not touched)

- `breakpoints.js`: after the name only `and (<condition>) [and (<condition>)…]` is valid. A comma (`@mobile, print`) and any other form
  (`@mobile (scripting: enabled)`, `@mobile print`, `@mobile and print`, `@mobile and`, `@mobile or (…)`) throw
  `atRule.error(…)` with the at-rule name and the params text. A bare `@mobile` stays valid.
- `hover.js`: `:hover` inside `:is()`, `:where()`, `:not()` or `:matches()` throws with the selector and a hint (write the hover selector on its own in the
  list); the exact `:not(:hover)` stays unwrapped as before. A rule inside a manual `@media` that has the `(hover: hover)` condition joined only by `and`
  is not wrapped again.
- Style (`style.md`): no bare `'media'` and `'atrule'`, they become named constants in `vite/constants.js`; no nested calls in a `return`.
- Verified without tests: a throwaway script in the scratch folder runs postcss with the three plugins over synthetic CSS (each bad form → its message, each good
  form → its output); `dist/**/*.css` is compared with the build before the change (identical).
- No new record: css.md says "more conditions follow the name", `postcss-notations` the hover wrapping; both stay, css.md gets one line each.
