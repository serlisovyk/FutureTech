# Plan: the Home hero (MY-263)

Branch `my-263-home-hero`. No spec: the section has no behavior, it is markup, styles and links. Records cited:
`folders-and-barrels` (where the section and its parts live), `borders-inside` (the dividers), `fluid-ramps` (every
size), `image-formats` (the decor goes through `Photo`). minista API: none new; `Photo` and `Icon` are the existing
wrappers, `fetchPriority` reaches the `<img>` through `Photo`.

## Where things live (decided here)

- `sections/home/Hero/`: `Hero.jsx` (the section), `HeroFeature.jsx` (one link card of the strip), `Hero.css`,
  `index.jsx`; a new group barrel `sections/home/index.jsx`, re-exported by `sections/index.jsx`. The strip under the
  hero is part of the Hero section (the design draws it in the same frame); it is not the Features section (MY-264).
- `data/hero.js` holds the hero texts (eyebrow, title, paragraph, the aside title, text, link and avatars, the
  title id); the stats are the first three of `data/stats.js`, the strip is `heroFeatures`.
- `pages/index.jsx` keeps `metadata`, drops the placeholder `h1` and renders `<Hero />`; the `h1` lives in the hero
  (`Heading` level 1, `size="hero"`).
- `Heading` gets the size `hero` (70 / 55 / 30, line height 1.2): a heading size the kit lacked, written in `Heading.css`.
- `AvatarGroup` gets `isCapsule`: the hero's capsule (radius 100, `Dark/10`, border, avatars 60 / 50 / 40, overlap 14) is
  a variant of the kit, shown on `/ui` next to the rounded box.
- The decor is `assets/images/decor/hero-decor.png`, already committed (the clipped result at the Desktop crop, 653x755
  at 2x): its rotation and the burst origin match the design. It fills the aside as `object-fit: cover` anchored top
  left, so at Laptop and Mobile the burst is a little larger than drawn and never leaves the column empty.

## Layout

- Hero = a row `[content | aside]` with the container's end padding, then the strip under it. `content` is a column
  (block padding 150 / 100 / 40 at the top only), the intro (eyebrow, `h1`, paragraph) and the stats row, which
  sits flush at the bottom; the aside is a column with the decor behind it and the avatar capsule, the title, the text
  and the outline `Button` to `/resources` at its bottom. Below 1024 px they stack, the stats stay three columns.
- Dividers: the stats row has a line above it, the aside a line before it (side by side only), the strip a line above
  it; the stats and the strip cards are equal grid columns with a line centred in the column gap
  (`::before` of every item after the first, the strip's lines are horizontal under 768 px, inset by the container).
- The strip card is one link: icon (50 / 40 / 30), title and subtitle, the yellow round arrow (52 / 44 / 40), and the
  caption below. Its links come from data (`/news`, `/podcasts`, `/resources`).
- Heading order: `h1`, then `h2` for the aside title and for each strip title.

## Deviations from the design (reported)

- At 1920 the design draws the capsule without border and with `Dark/08`, and no line before the aside or above the
  strip; the other two widths and the design notes draw them: built the same at every width.
- The first strip card's caption is 14 at 390 and the other two 16: 14 everywhere.
- The aside's own left line at 390 sits on the screen edge: not drawn when stacked.
