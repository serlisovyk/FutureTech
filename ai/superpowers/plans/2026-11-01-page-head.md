# Plan: the page head as components (MY-305)

Branch `my-305-page-head`. No spec: the built `<head>` of every page and article does not change. No new decision
record: the layout still builds the head from the page's `metadata`, `url` and `post` (`shared-page-head`); only the
list of tags moves out of the layout. Where the parts live follows `folders-and-barrels`.

## Checked against the docs

minista's Head guide (<https://minista.dev/docs/guide/head>) and `minista agents`: `Head` renders nothing where it
stands, it collects its elements while the page renders and adds them to the layout's `<head>`; it may be used from
any child component; elements from several `Head`s are all collected, in render order. So a component that wraps its
tags in `Head` and sits inside the layout's `<head>` prints them in the same place as today. The preloads, `<title>` and
the script are plain tags of the layout's `<head>`, not `Head`; the preload component keeps that.

## Changes

- `components/head/`, a new group by role (the page's intro stays in `page/`), one folder per component with `X.jsx` and
  `index.jsx` (no stylesheet: they print no markup): `FontPreloads` (the two preloads), `SeoMeta` (description, robots,
  canonical), `BrowserMeta` (theme colour, the two icons), `SocialMeta` (Open Graph, Twitter; the page's `title`,
  `description`, `canonicalUrl` and `post`), `ArticleMeta` (the article's `published_time` and `author`, rendered by
  `SocialMeta` when there is a `post`), `JsonLd` (`Organization`, and `BlogPosting` for a `post`). The group has an
  `index.jsx` and `components/index.jsx` re-exports it.
- `assets/fonts/index.js` exports `interRegular` and `kumbhSansMedium`; `FontPreloads` imports `from '@assets/fonts'`.
  Icons and images get no barrel: minista reads their root paths.
- `layouts/index.jsx` keeps `<html>`, the `<title>`, the script, the components in today's order, and `<body>` as it is.
  `socialTitle` and the social image move into `SocialMeta` and `JsonLd` (`getSocialMeta` is pure and cheap).
- Imports stay `@data/site.js` and `@utils`: the `@data` barrel and `@constants` come with MY-303, which runs at the same
  time; the rebase resolves the import lines.
