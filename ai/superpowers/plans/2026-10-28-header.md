# Plan: the header (MY-257)

Branch `my-257-header`; behaviour in `ai/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-header.md`. Records cited: `fluid-ramps`,
`page-head-and-entries`, `oop-javascript`, `styles-next-to-components`, `path-aliases`, `design-is-a-reference`.
minista API used, checked against the guide `minista agents` prints and the installed types
(`plugins/ssg/types.d.ts`): the layout receives the page's `url` (`/`, `/news`, no trailing slash) next to
`title` and `children`; `Svg` and `Sprite` (through `Icon`) read root paths.

## Decided (author)

- The open menu is a drawer from the right over a dimmed page; `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the button;
  it closes on `Escape`, the backdrop, a link click; scroll locked; focus inside, back on the button; no motion
  under reduced motion; the drawer holds the links and Contact Us. Burger below 768 px (the design shows the full
  navbar at 1440 and the glyph at 390; `width < 768px` is the structural query).
- The strip at 390 has 14 px block padding (the design's 40 is room for a status bar).
- Borders are inside, as in the design: a block's padding is the design value minus its border, so the outer
  size is the drawn one. The UI-kit components are not touched.
- No hover looks (a later task).

## Structure

| Piece                                             | Where                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Header` (skip link, strip, navbar, menu) + css   | `sections/shared/Header.jsx`, `Header.css`; the layout renders it on every page |
| Close glyph                                       | `assets/icons/close.svg` (the design's solid x-mark, Heroicons 24 grid)         |
| `Toggle`: `data-toggle-dismiss`, `onToggle`       | `scripts/components/Toggle.js`                                                  |
| `HeaderController`: inert page, focus, breakpoint | `scripts/controllers/HeaderController.js`, row in `main.js`, query in constants |

- The layout passes `url` as `currentPath`; the active link is the one whose `href` equals it. The links come from
  `data/navigation.js`: items without `isAction` are the nav, the one with `isAction` is the `Button`.
- **One menu element, two looks.** The wrapper `data-toggle-target` is `display: contents` from 768 px (the nav and
  the button sit in the navbar grid `1fr auto 1fr`, which centres the nav on the page as the design does) and a
  fixed overlay below it: a backdrop (`data-toggle-dismiss`) and the drawer. No second copy of the links.
- The menu is `hidden` in the markup, which is what the closed state is before the script runs. Without script
  (`@media (scripting: none)`) and from 768 px the stylesheet overrides `hidden`, so the links are never lost.
  Opening and closing animate with `@starting-style` and a discrete `display` transition, so no script writes
  styles; the duration is `--transition-duration`.
- Scroll lock is CSS: `:root:has(.header__menu:not([hidden]))` under the mobile query.
- `Toggle` stays generic. It gains elements with `data-toggle-dismiss` (a click closes and returns focus to the
  button, like `Escape`) and `onToggle(handler)`, which reports the state. `HeaderController` composes a `Toggle`
  on the header, marks everything outside the drawer `inert` while open, moves focus to the close button, and
  closes when the viewport leaves `(width < 768px)` (`MEDIA.mobileMenu` in `constants.js`, equal to the stylesheet).
- The newsletter copy and the labels are unique to this section and stay in `Header.jsx`.

## Values (design numbers, 1920 / 1440 / 390)

- Strip: text 18 / 14 / 12 Grey/60, arrow 24 / 20 / 20 Yellow/55, gap 10, inline padding 24, block padding

  Contact Us is the `Button`.
- Plate: padding 14 x 24 / 10 x 18, radius 10 / 6, 1px `Dark/20`. With borders inside, the CSS padding is one less.
