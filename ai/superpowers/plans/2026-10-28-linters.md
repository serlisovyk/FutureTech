## Decisions made here

- **ESLint stays on 9** (`9.39.5`, with `@eslint/js` of the same version): both plugins declare peers up to
  ESLint 9 only, so 10 does not install without `--legacy-peer-deps`.
- **lint-staged 16.4.0**, not 17: 17 needs Node >= 22.22.1, above the repo's `engines`; 16 needs >= 20.17.
- **No `.eslintignore` / `.stylelintignore`:** ignores live in the configs.
- **Nothing beyond the listed rule sets:** no import, no unicorn, no extra Stylelint plugins (scope rule: more
  rules are a new decision).
