# Plan: the news card and the video card (MY-260)

Branch `my-260-cards`. Records cited: `folders-and-barrels`, `borders-inside`, `fluid-ramps`, `image-formats`,
`shared-page-head`, `ui-page-and-size-limits`. minista API used: `Picture` through `Photo` (`sizes`, `breakpoints`),
`Sprite` through `Icon`; checked against the installed package (`minista agents` and the types of its image plugin):
`sizes` is the standard `sizes` string, `breakpoints` the list of widths the plugin renders. No spec: the cards are
static markup; the only things a visitor does (follow a link, press a button) are plain elements.

## Scope

A new component group `components/cards/` (its own `index.jsx`, re-exported by the root barrel), two cards, three
helpers, the shared data they need, eight photos and the `/ui` demo.

| Part                  | What                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `NewsCard`            | photo, title, category, two `CounterPill`s (likes, comments), a "Read More" outline link to the article                       |
| `VideoCard`           | preview with the ▶ button and the duration, title, text; `isEpisode` adds the "Listen Podcast" button and the episode metrics |
| `getPostUrl`          | `utils/get-post-url.js`: `/blog/<slug>`; the article route reuses it                                                          |
| `formatCount`         | `utils/format-count.js`: `24500` becomes `24.5k`, `60` stays `60`                                                             |
| `getPostsByPlacement` | `utils/get-posts-by-placement.js`: the posts of one placement (`trending`); News and `/ui` use it                             |
| `getCategoryName`     | `utils/get-category-name.js`: the display name of a post's category slug, from both category lists in `data/categories.js`    |

## Decided (author, in the task)

- The article URL is `/blog/<slug>`, built by `getPostUrl`; the card calls it, the caller passes the slug.
- The ▶ is a real `<button type="button" data-video-id data-video-title aria-label="Play: <title>">`; `videoId` is read
  from data as is, no click handler (the video modal is MY-262, on its own branch).
- Counters are numbers in data and are formatted by a helper, not in JSX.
- Padding is the design's value minus the border (`borders-inside`); no hover styles (MY-284).
- Photos are copied from the design export into `assets/images/photos/` under the names the data already uses.
- A size-limit overflow is reported, `.size-limit.json` is not touched here.

## Decided (in this plan, no author answer needed)

- **Counters are likes and comments**, as the design draws them (heart, then the speech bubble); the issue says "likes,
  shares". The data has both numbers and the design's second pill carries the comment count (`60`), so the card takes
  `likes` and `comments`. Swapping to `shares` is one prop and one icon.
- **One link per card.** "Read More" is the `<a href>`; the title is a plain heading, so a screen reader meets one
  link, named "Read More about <title>" through a visually hidden part. The title is an `h3`: both places the card
  appears sit under an `h2`.
- **The ▶ button is part of `VideoCard`'s folder** (`VideoCardPlay.jsx`), not a `Button` variant: it is an icon button
  with a data contract nothing else uses. Its stylesheet is `VideoCard.css`, its classes `video-card__play`.
- **`isEpisode` is one prop and one modifier** for the two looks the design draws: the video has a 24 / 20 / 18 title
  and the episode 22 / 18 / 16 with a looser stack and the "Listen Podcast" button. A separate component would copy
  the preview.
- **"Listen Podcast" has no action yet.** The design draws it and gives it no destination: episodes have no page, and a
  `href="#"` is forbidden. It is rendered as the kit's outline `Button` (a `<button>` with no handler) and the open
  question goes to the author: open the player like ▶, or link somewhere.
- **`videoId` for episodes.** `data/podcasts.js` episodes have none; each gets the same placeholder the videos have
  (`placeholder-video-id`) so the ▶ contract is complete. The video modal task fills real ids in both files.
- **Photo sizing is by height, not aspect ratio**: the design's photo heights are 222 / 185 / 185 (news), 412 / 331 /
  210 (video), 290 / 244 / 208 (episode), each at columns of different widths, so the photo is `width: 100%` with a
  fluid `height` and `object-fit: cover`. The overlay gradient (transparent from 44% to `Dark/08`) is a pseudo-element
  of the preview.
- **`sizes` are constants in `data/card-photos.js`** (one per card kind, from the column widths of the 3 / 2 / 3
  column grids: 512 / 407 / 358 for news, 718 / 590 / 358 for video, 471 / 387 / 358 for episodes) and all three share the
  widths 360, 720 and 1080 (`cardPhotoBreakpoints`), since a card photo is never wider than 718 px (1436 at 2x, and
  the sources stop at 1080).
- **Sources are JPEG**, 4:4:4 q92, capped at 1080 px wide (`ai-woman-code-reflection` is 1380 in the export); the others
  keep their size (626 to 1060).
- **Only the photos `/ui` renders are copied**: the three trending posts, the first two videos and the first three
  episodes (eight files). The sections that render the rest copy theirs.
- **No new token**: the card gaps and sizes are each one block's and are written in it; the shared values come from the
  tokens that exist (`--radius-card`, `--border`, `--font-size-button`, `--font-size-card-title`, `--font-size-lead`).

## Steps

1. `data/`: `card-photos.js`; `videoId` on episodes; the demo lists in `ui-kit.js` if the real lists do not fit.
2. `utils/`: the four helpers and their lines in `index.js`.
3. `components/cards/NewsCard/` and `components/cards/VideoCard/` with their stylesheets and `index.jsx`; the group's
   `index.jsx`; one line in `components/index.jsx`.
4. The photos.
5. `/ui`: a `NewsCard` group (the three trending posts in a 3-column grid) and a `VideoCard` group (two videos in a
   2-column grid, three episodes in a 3-column grid), the grids being the demo's own styles in `UiKit.css`.
6. `ai/map.md`; `ai/design-system.md` only if a number there is wrong.
